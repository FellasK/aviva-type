# Privacy Policy — AvivaType

**Last updated:** October 2026

## Overview

AvivaType ("the Extension") is a browser extension that plays keyboard sounds while typing.

## Data Collection

**AvivaType does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data.**

All settings (sound theme, volume, blocked sites, UI theme) are stored locally in your browser using the `chrome.storage.sync` API, which syncs across your devices via your browser account. No data is sent to any external server.

## Permissions Used

| Permission | Reason |
|---|---|
| `storage` | Save your preferences (volume, theme, blocked sites) |
| `tabs` | Read the active tab's hostname for the site-mute feature |

## Third-Party Services

None. The Extension does not communicate with any external service, analytics platform, or API.
